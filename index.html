02 A short history of not getting left behind

Six tools that changed business

01 / 06

  1. Prehistory

    Fire

    The first technology. The ones who learned to carry it ate better, slept safer and went further. Nobody remembers the holdouts.

  2. c. 3500 BC

    The wheel

    Suddenly distance was negotiable. Trade stopped being limited to what you could carry on your back.

  3. c. 1440

    The press

    Ideas learned to scale. One page became ten thousand, and whoever published first set the conversation.

  4. 1879

    Electricity

    The workday stopped ending at sundown. The shops that wired up outpaced the ones that waited to see how it played out.

  5. 1991

    The web

    Every business got a front door open to the whole world. Some are still deciding whether to unlock it.

  6. Now

    Intelligence

    Software that reads, writes, answers and acts. The gap between the businesses using it and the ones watching is opening right now. This is where we come in.
